How it works

From first chat to final key

Four clear stages, one dedicated team and a fixed timeline agreed before work begins.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We listen, measure your space and agree a budget and brief together.

  2. 2

    Concept & 3D Design

    Mood boards, material samples and photo-real 3D renders, revised until they feel right.

  3. 3

    Execution

    Our own crews build to plan, with weekly site updates and quality checks.

  4. 4

    Handover

    A deep clean and styled reveal, then your keys, warranty and care guide.
